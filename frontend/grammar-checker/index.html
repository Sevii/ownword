Free grammar checker

Find spelling, grammar, and punctuation mistakes, plus wordy phrases and repeated words. The checker runs in your browser, so your text stays on your device.

Free, no account. Opens the editor with your text.

What it checks

  • Spelling, including words added to your personal dictionary
  • Grammar and common confusions, such as “their” and “they’re”
  • Punctuation and capitalization
  • Optional style notes: wordy phrases, repeated words, long sentences, passive voice, and hedging

How it is different

Mistakes are underlined as you type. Click one to see the fix, apply it, and undo it if you change your mind. Likely errors and optional style notes are shown separately, so you can ignore the style notes when they don’t fit.

Checking runs locally. Nothing you type is uploaded unless you choose an AI action with your own AI account.
