Sentence rewriter

Make a sentence clearer, shorter, or more formal, then see exactly what changed before you accept it.

Rewriting uses your own AI account or a model on your machine; checking is free.

How it works

  • Select the sentence in the editor and choose Clarify, Shorten, Fix grammar, or Adjust tone, or describe the change you want.
  • The rewrite appears as a diff: removed words struck through, added words highlighted. Nothing changes until you choose Apply.
  • A meaning check warns when a rewrite changes amounts, dates, names, negations, or conditions.

Where the text goes

Only the sentence you selected is sent, directly from your browser to the AI you connected: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, an OpenAI-compatible server such as Ollama on your own computer, or a small model running inside the browser with nothing sent anywhere.
