Paraphrasing tool you can check

Rewrite a passage to be clearer, shorter, or in a different tone, then see exactly what changed before you accept it.

Rewriting uses your own AI account; grammar checking is free.

How it works

  • Choose Clarify, Shorten, Fix grammar, or Adjust tone, or say what you want in your own words.
  • Every change is shown as a diff. Nothing is applied until you choose Apply, and one Undo reverses it.
  • A meaning check warns when a rewrite changes amounts, dates, names, negations, or conditions.
  • Bold, italic, and links are kept.

Your AI account

Rewriting uses an AI model from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or OpenRouter, with your own API key. The key stays in your browser. Each request goes directly from your browser to the provider you chose.
