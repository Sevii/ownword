Reading the score

  • 70 and above: easy, like a popular magazine. Most emails and web pages should land here.
  • 50 to 70: fairly standard, like a newspaper. Fine for reports and documentation.
  • 30 to 50: fairly difficult, like academic writing. Expect readers to slow down.
  • Below 30: difficult. Shorter sentences and shorter words raise the score fastest.

The formula rewards short sentences and short words. Short samples swing a lot; aim for a hundred words or more.
