Privacy and data

ownword is built so your writing stays with you. This page explains exactly what goes where.

Where your data goes

WhatWhere it goesStored
Your writing, while you proofreadNowhere. Checking runs in your browser.In your browser only (IndexedDB), with recovery copies
Text you send to an AI actionDirectly from your browser to the AI provider you connectedThe provider handles it under its own terms
Your AI API keySent with each AI request, in a request header, directly to the providerSaved in this browser (not encrypted). Remove it in Settings on shared devices

Your drafts

Drafts live in this browser’s storage. Clearing site data deletes them, and some browsers, such as Safari, may remove storage for sites you have not visited in a while. Export anything you want to keep. There are no accounts and no cloud sync.

AI actions

AI runs only when you choose an action, on the text you chose: a selection or the whole document. The request names the provider and model, and every result says which model produced it. Your provider may retain requests under its own policy: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter. Cancelling a request stops it here, but the provider may already have processed or billed it.

No analytics

ownword sends no usage counts or feedback. There is no application server, account, or cloud sync. Static hosting may have ordinary access logs for downloading app files.
